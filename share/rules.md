## Delegation (token saving)
- Hand low-reasoning chores (code surveys and grep sweeps, boilerplate, tests from a spec, mechanical refactors and renames, lint/type fixes, docs, log triage) to a cheap model with `delegate -C <dir> "<task>"`. Follow the `delegate` skill for the task format, verification and escalation.
- Do not spawn subagents on your own model for those chores.
- Keep planning, architecture, ambiguous debugging, security and final diff review yourself.
